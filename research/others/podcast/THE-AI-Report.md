## Episode 995
- Title: How to Use AI to Rank #1 on Google for SEO
- Date: 2025-02-14
- Guest(s): Tom Winter (Co-founder of SEOwind)
### Show Notes (Excerpt)
In this episode, Liam Lawson and Tom Winter discuss how AI is fundamentally changing SEO, content strategy, and search engine rankings as we head into 2025. They cover the evolution of AI workflows, the rise of autonomous AI agents, the impact on the job market, and how businesses can maintain authority in an AI-driven search landscape.
### Key Takeaways
- AI is shifting SEO from manual execution to workflow orchestration and system design.
- Autonomous AI agents are becoming practical for repeatable SEO and content tasks.
- Authority and trust signals remain critical even as AI-generated content scales.
- Teams should adapt skills toward strategy, validation, and quality control in AI-assisted pipelines.
