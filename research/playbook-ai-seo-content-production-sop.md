#AI SEO CONTENT PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK

## Where experts disagree

### 1) Content creation: Repurpose vs create new

- **What Author A recommends:** Matt Diggity says first improve and reuse your best existing content, then think about new content (source: Matt Diggity, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fK3OaJ9Mjk , 02.03.2026).
- **What Author B recommends:** Kasra Dash says create new content in missing topic areas found through competitor gap analysis (source: Kasra Dash, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Velm1lByhzs , 10.04.2026).
- **Which side I take and why:** I prefer a mixed approach, but I lean more toward Kasra Dash because short-term gains can come from repurposing, while long-term growth needs full topic coverage (source: Kasra Dash, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBo9Est1Y6E , 12.04.2026; source: Matt Diggity, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fK3OaJ9Mjk , 02.03.2026).

### 2) Speed vs risk in visibility expansion

- **What Author A recommends:** Jesper Nissen supports fast expansion across many platforms and entity profiles to increase visibility quickly (source: Jesper Nissen, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHNl0PiDce4 , 26.02.2026).
- **What Author B recommends:** Ahrefs supports steady and clean growth with strong technical SEO, structured content, and gradual authority building (source: Ahrefs Tutorials, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DnRCo2vvKvY , 12.02.2026).
- **Which side I take and why:** I side with Ahrefs because it is safer for long-term brands and has lower risk of poor-quality signals from over-aggressive expansion (source: Ahrefs Tutorials, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DnRCo2vvKvY , 12.02.2026).

### 3) SEO strategy emphasis: Off-site signals vs on-site messaging

- **What Author A recommends:** Nathan Gotch says off-site mentions and citations are very important for AI visibility (source: Nathan Gotch, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mB0QuXEv_sg , 30.03.2026).
- **What Author B recommends:** Neil Patel says clear on-page messaging matters because traffic is useless if users do not convert (source: Neil Patel, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/neilkpatel_googleads-marketingtips-copywritingtips-ugcPost-7449788736172761088-g1wa , 14.04.2026).
- **Which side I take and why:** I lean toward Neil Patel, but include Gotch's layer, because business results come from conversion and not just visibility (source: Neil Patel, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7449619042056802304/ , 13.04.2026; source: Nathan Gotch, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAJL2ob_Lks , 13.03.2026).

## What I rejected and why

1. **Over-aggressive entity expansion as default (Jesper Nissen).** I rejected this as a standard rule because moving too fast can create spam-like signals, inconsistent profiles, and brand-risk problems for small or compliance-focused companies (source: Jesper Nissen, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHNl0PiDce4 , 26.02.2026; source: Jesper Nissen, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cffV4SBbpiw , 19.02.2026).

2. **AI workflows without human QA. (Josh Crouch)** I rejected this as a standard rule because fully unreviewed AI output can become generic, reduce brand voice quality, and hurt trust; this playbook keeps human review checkpoints (source: Josh Crouch, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7449805148563865600/ , 14.04.2026; source: Liam Lawson (guest Tom Winter), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=utlt-kDCb_Y , 14.02.2025).

## My original ideas

1. **AI Citation Hijacking Strategy (original idea).**
   - Step 1: Check AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI) and note which websites are getting cited for your target keywords.
   - Step 2: Create a better page on the same topic using simpler language, better structure, latest data, and stronger proof.
   - Step 3: Update and improve that page regularly so AI systems see it as fresh and useful.

**Why it could work (simple):**
- AI tools usually trust and repeat a set of known sources for a topic.
- If your page gives better value (clear explanation, good format, latest info), AI tools may start citing your page instead.
- Over time, this can help you replace weaker sources and increase your brand visibility in AI answers (source: Nathan Gotch, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mB0QuXEv_sg , 30.03.2026; source: Ahrefs Tutorials, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DnRCo2vvKvY , 12.02.2026).

## Weaknesses of this playbook

- AI search behavior is changing fast, so what works today may not work the same way after a few months.
- Many steps need tools and clean data, which may be difficult for small teams with low budgets.
- This playbook mixes SEO, CRO, AI workflows, and ops, so execution can feel heavy for solo founders.
- Results can take time, so testing and learning cycles may be slow.
- Regulated industries (finance, health, legal) may need extra checks not fully covered here.
