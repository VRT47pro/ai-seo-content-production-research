# AI SEO Content Production Research

This repository contains source collection for AI-driven SEO research across YouTube, LinkedIn, and podcast channels. The goal is to study how active practitioners apply AI in SEO, content workflows, visibility strategy, and performance measurement.

## What I Collected

- `research/sources.md`
  - Master index of all experts with links, collection dates, and brief annotations.
- `research/youtube-transcripts/`
  - YouTube transcripts organized by video (grouped by creator folder).
  - Current coverage: 5 creators x 5 videos each (25 transcript files).
- `research/linkdin-posts/`
  - Recent LinkedIn posts organized by author.
  - Current coverage: 3 experts, 2 posts per expert.
- `research/others/podcast/`
  - Podcast episode notes, summaries, and key takeaways.
  - Current coverage: 2 podcast sources.

## Why These Experts Were Chosen

Experts were selected based on practical execution, not theory-only commentary. Selection criteria:

- They actively publish applied SEO/AI workflows, not only high-level opinions.
- They show real implementation examples (content systems, AI search visibility, attribution, CRO, operations).
- They operate in different environments (agency, founder-led, content platform, local/service business contexts).
- They provide current material relevant to AI-era search behavior (AI Overviews, LLM visibility, zero-click impact, workflow automation).

This mix improves coverage across technical SEO, semantic SEO, AI-assisted content production, reporting/attribution, and demand-generation execution.
